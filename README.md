# The Aviloop Descent

An interactive telling of the Aviloop story, built as a Halloween exhibit.

A single self-contained HTML page. No build step, no dependencies, no tracking.
Open `index.html` and it runs.

## Controls

- `space` / arrow down: descend a level
- `N`: presenter notes
- `E`: strike out every unproven claim
- `M`: mute

Audio is synthesised in the browser and starts on your first click or keypress.

## About the content

Claims are colour coded: green is documented, orange is an allegation or a
disputed report, red is internet rumour with nothing behind it.

Nadia Marcinko has never been charged with a crime. Reporting has described her
as possibly both a victim and an accomplice, and her lawyers say Epstein
severely traumatised her. The women in the 2011 adverts were identified by later
research as adults, and at least one has no connection to any of this beyond a
modelling job. Their faces are not used on this page. Nothing here is a finding
of guilt.

The three people shown in costume are friends who agreed to appear.
